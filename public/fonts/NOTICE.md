# Portfolio typefaces

PP Editorial Old Light and PP Neue Montreal Regular/Medium are by Pangram Pangram Foundry.

These fonts are used only for Jake Worsham's personal portfolio, not the product demos. The foundry explicitly lists personal web portfolios among its free personal-use examples: https://pangrampangram.com/pages/faq

Typeface information: https://pangrampangram.com/products/editorial-old and https://pangrampangram.com/products/neue-montreal

The webfont builds were identified in the user-supplied reference's public CSS at https://khanhnguyen.design/. Typeface ownership remains with the foundry. These are not project-authored or open-source assets; do not reuse them in commercial client work without the applicable license.
